# Final report outline

Phase 1 evidence is closed and `validation-v1` remains frozen. Phase 2 provides a prediction
contract, six deterministic baselines, provenance-safe horizon examples, one fixed LightGBM
benchmark, a seven-run comparison, and an educational walkthrough. The first Phase 2G candidate
was uploaded on 2026-09-04 and received a user-reported Zindi public score of 0.778144248 at rank
320; operational deployment evidence remains pending.

1. **Problem and operational context** — global next-month TWS forecasting, intended users, and
   unresolved operational publication timing.
2. **Data and prediction-time availability** — 2,154,021 Train rows, 280,961 Test rows, 15,715
   shared locations, discontinuous monthly coverage, schemas, and identifier construction from
   `docs/data_dictionary.md` and `docs/phase1a_structural_audit.md`.
3. **Leakage prevention** — exact calendar joining, event/release cutoffs, immutable observed-TWS
   provenance, fold-local fitting, mask independence, validation uniqueness, policy isolation,
   and artifact compatibility. See `docs/leakage_threat_register.md` and the Phase 2A contract.
4. **Validation methodology** — validation-v1 fits once at F01=`2004-09` and F02=`2008-12`, then
   simulates the Test-shaped mask under observed-only, recursion-disabled policy. F01 retains
   278,059 rows and F02 273,906; all 551,965 pooled target keys are distinct with zero fold overlap.
5. **Baselines** — global/location means, climatology, persistence, seasonal naive, and
   trend-seasonal references from Phase 2B. Persistence is strongest deterministic at 0.673722;
   fallback frequencies and diagnostics remain in compact metrics.
6. **Model architecture** — fixed two-thread LightGBM GBDT: 200 rounds, learning rate 0.05,
   31 leaves, depth 8, 13 numeric features, native missing handling, no tuning or early stopping.
   Fold-local inputs use exact `y-h` TWS and `y-1` covariates.
7. **Innovation** — pending evidence against the official rubric; do not claim novelty from a
   standard baseline system.
8. **Results** — LightGBM pooled OOF RMSE 0.592987 versus persistence 0.673722, improvement
   0.080735 (11.983%), improving both folds. Fold, horizon, mask, latitude, and SPEI values come
   from `reports/phase2e_comparison.json` and referenced compact metrics.
9. **Robustness** — LightGBM fold gap is 0.001191. Masked and long-horizon rows are weaker;
   `[-60,-30)` and `[-30,0)` latitude bands require cautious discussion. Two-fold selection and
   spatially non-uniform exclusions remain limitations.
10. **Practical deployment** — current-covariate latency remains unresolved; no operational claim
    without evidence.
11. **Reproducibility and resources** — frozen configurations, seed 20260904, exact-calendar
    provenance, deterministic 2,000,000-row sampling, seven registry records, ignored-artifact
    hashes, and README/notebook commands. Runs used about 81--145 seconds and 2.1--2.3 GiB peak.
12. **References and artifact manifest** — Phase 2B metrics, Phase 2C manifest, Phase 2D metrics,
    Phase 2E comparison, registry, and `notebooks/01_baseline_walkthrough.ipynb`.

## Phase 2 limitations, rejected approaches, and Phase 3 hypotheses

Limitations include two historical folds, complete-path selection effects, capped training,
long-horizon and masked-input degradation, uneven spatial/SPEI-bin performance, and unresolved
current-covariate timing. Rejected approaches include random splitting, row-position joins, later
backfill, recursion, unsafe full expansion, implicit row dropping, tuning sweeps, validation early
stopping, external data, AutoML, and paid compute. Phase 3 hypotheses are limited to auditable
horizon-specific calibration and bounded features for masked/long-horizon cases; they are not yet
findings.
